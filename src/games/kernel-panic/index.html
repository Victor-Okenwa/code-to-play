About this game

What it is

A vertical shmup. Fly a craft, shoot falling threats, and unlock spaceships with gold and diamonds. Survive until HP is gone. Rush-hour alien craft and bosses interrupt the fight. One play per run.

Difficulty

  • Easy — segfaults only at first, slow start, 3 HP. Rush every 20s. Bosses every 8–15s.
  • Medium — zombies from the start, faster spawn, 3 HP. Rush every 15s.
  • Hard — memory leaks that split on hit, avoidable healthy processes, 2 HP, panic waves from the start. Rush every 15s.

How to play

Pick a difficulty, choose a craft, then Boot Kernel. Score 10 / 15 / 20 for segfault, zombie, and leak kills. Kills drop gold (and sometimes diamonds). Do not shoot healthy processes (−25).

  • Arrows / WASD — Move.
  • Space — Fire.
  • P — Pause. Ctrl+M — Mute. Ctrl+F — Focus play.
  • Counter rush lasers with your shots; the alien craft takes 5 hits to destroy.
  • Bosses: Blue 30 / Yellow 90 / Red 180 HP; drop 3 diamonds + 6 gold.

Power-ups

Threat kills have a chance to drop a colored capsule. Collect it. Timed mods last a few seconds and can overlap; grabbing the same kind again refreshes the timer. Health heals on collect.

  • Grey shield — ignore enemy body hits.
  • Red rapid fire — shoot faster.
  • Magenta spread shot — fire three bolts.
  • Blue weaker enemies — threats slow down.
  • Yellow score boost — threat kills score double.
  • Green health — restore 1 HP.

How to Play

Controls

Rules

Power-ups

Collect falling capsules. About 1 in 3 threat kills drop one:

  • Grey — shield (ignore body hits)
  • Red — rapid fire
  • Magenta — spread shot
  • Blue — slower threats
  • Yellow — double score on threat kills
  • Green — heal 1 HP
