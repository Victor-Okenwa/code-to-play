About this game

What it is

A vertical shmup. You fly the kernel. Threats fall from the top — segfaults, zombie processes, and memory leaks. Last 60 seconds or until HP is gone. One play per run.

Difficulty

  • Easy — segfaults only, slow spawn, 3 HP.
  • Medium — adds zombie processes, faster spawn, 3 HP.
  • Hard — memory leaks that split on hit, avoidable healthy processes (green, dashed), 2 HP, panic waves.

How to play

Pick a difficulty, then Choose Difficulty. Press Boot Kernel. Score 10 / 15 / 20 for segfault, zombie, and leak kills. Panic waves add a bonus. Shooting a healthy process is −25 (floor 0).

  • Arrows / WASD — Move.
  • Space — Fire.
  • P — Pause. Ctrl+M — Mute. Ctrl+F — Focus play.
  • Do not shoot green dashed processes. They are healthy.
  • Memory leaks split into smaller segfaults when hit.
