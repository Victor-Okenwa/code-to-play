About this game

What it is

One-button flyer with a CI twist. Flap through red gates labeled lint, test, typecheck, and deploy. Collect gold coins and rare red diamonds to unlock birds. Rush hour lets leftover gates scroll off as coin waves enter from the right — then bug bots lock on and fire three shots.

Difficulty

  • Easy — Wide gaps, slow scroll, rush every 20s. Speed and gap tightness ramp as you last. Gravity stays put.
  • Medium — Faster pipeline, rush every 15s. Same ramp, still constant gravity.
  • Hard — Narrow gates, more diamonds, rush every 10s.

How to play

Pick a difficulty, choose a bird, then Start Pipeline. One play is spent only on the final crash. Extra lives from some birds resurrect you once without spending another play.

  • Space, Arrow Up, or click — Flap.
  • Arrow Down — Descend.
  • P — Pause. Ctrl+M — Mute. Ctrl+F — Focus play.
  • Gold is common. Diamonds are rare, and less rare on Hard.
  • During rush hour, dodge the three bug-bot shots after the warning.

How to Play

Controls

  • Space, Arrow Up, or click — Flap
  • Arrow Down — Descend
  • Left / Right — Cycle characters
  • P — Pause/Resume
  • Ctrl+M — Toggle sound
  • Ctrl+F — Toggle focus play mode

Rules

  • Fly through the green gap in each CI gate
  • Collect gold often; diamonds are rare
  • Rush hour streams coin shapes, then bug bots fire three shots
  • Hit a pipe, a shot, the ceiling, or the floor and you lose a life
