About this game

What it is

One-button flyer with a CI twist. Flap a teal deploy bird through scrolling red gates labeled lint, test, typecheck, and deploy. Each green gap is a passing check. Hit a pipe, the ceiling, or the floor and the pipeline goes red.

Difficulty

  • Easy — Soft gravity, wide gaps, slow scroll. Best for a short break.
  • Hard — Tighter gates and a faster pipeline.
  • Very Hard — Narrow gaps, heavy gravity, and the fastest scroll.

How to play

Pick a difficulty, then Choose Difficulty. Press Start Pipeline. One play is spent when the build fails. High scores are stored per difficulty on this machine.

  • Space or click — Flap.
  • P — Pause. Ctrl+M — Mute. Ctrl+F — Focus play.
  • Pass through the green window to score a check.
  • Hit a red gate, the ceiling, or the floor and the build fails.
