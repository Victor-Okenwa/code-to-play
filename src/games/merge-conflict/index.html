About this game

What it is

A git-themed match-3. The board is a 6×12 grid of hunks — labeled ours, theirs, and base (plus index and stash on harder settings). Swap neighbors so three or more of the same hunk line up. They merge and clear. New rows rise from the bottom. If hunks reach the top, HEAD diverges and the merge fails.

Difficulty

  • Easy — ours / theirs / base only. A new row every 6 seconds.
  • Medium — 4 hunk types (adds index). A new row every 4 seconds.
  • Hard — 5 hunk types (adds stash). A new row every 2.5 seconds.

How to play

Pick a difficulty, then Choose Difficulty. Press Start Merge. The white box is a two-cell cursor: the hunk under it and the one to its right. One play is spent when HEAD overflows.

  • Arrow keys — Move the cursor.
  • Enter — Swap those two cells. Space does not swap.
  • Space — Pause. Ctrl+M — Mute. Ctrl+F — Focus play.
  • You can swap a hunk with an empty cell so gravity drops it.
  • Match 3+ of the same label in a row or column to merge. Gravity then chains — those are rebase combos and score extra.
  • Score is hunks cleared × 10 × chain waves. Keep the board low. The top two rows are the danger zone.
