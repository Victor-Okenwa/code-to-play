About this game

What it is

A landscape single-lane runner (like the Chrome dino). Jump over trees, duck under iron bars, and stay ahead of the bug man. Unlock runners with coins and diamonds. Base HP is 1 — a hit means caught unless you have a shield or +1 HP from a runner or health pickup. One play per run.

Difficulty

  • Easy — slow scroll, 1 HP. Force Push every 20s.
  • Medium — mixed obstacles, 1 HP. Force Push every 15s.
  • Hard — fast scroll, 1 HP. Force Push every 15s.

How to play

Pick a difficulty, choose a runner (Bob Main … John Origin), then Start Run. Score rises with distance; coins and diamonds pad the wallet and the lead.

  • Up / W / Space — Jump over trees.
  • Down / S — Duck under hanging iron bars.
  • P — Pause. Ctrl+M — Mute. Ctrl+F — Focus play.
  • Force Push rushes dump coin snakes then dense waves — sometimes a dual push, sometimes HEAD.

Power-ups

Pickups drop along the track. Timed mods last a few seconds and refresh if you grab the same kind again. Shield and boost are one-shot.

  • Magnet — pull coins and diamonds.
  • Shield — absorb one hit.
  • Jetpack — lift into the sky for coin formations (Up/Down to fly).
  • Boost — auto-clear the next obstacle hit.
  • x2 — double coin rewards.
  • Health — restore 1 HP.
