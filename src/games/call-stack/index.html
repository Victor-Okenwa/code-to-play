How to Play

Controls

  • Left / Right — Move the falling frame
  • Up — Rotate (swap call / return)
  • Down — Soft drop
  • X — Hard drop
  • Space — Pause/Resume
  • Ctrl+M — Toggle sound
  • Ctrl+F — Toggle focus play mode

Rules

  • Each piece is a call stacked on a return, or the reverse
  • A return sitting on a matching-color call pops both frames
  • Chain pops for combo score. Touch the top row and you overflow
