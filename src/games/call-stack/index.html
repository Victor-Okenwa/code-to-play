About this game

What it is

A falling-block puzzle about the call stack. Each piece is two frames in one column: a filled fn (call) and an outlined ret (return), each in a color. Land a return on a matching-color call to pop both frames. Fill the top row and you overflow.

Difficulty

  • Easy — 3 colors, slow drop, 14-row stack.
  • Medium — 4 colors, faster drop, same 14-row depth.
  • Hard — 5 colors, fastest drop, 12-row stack so you overflow sooner.

How to play

Pick a difficulty, then Choose Difficulty. Press Start Debugging. Pieces spawn at the top and fall. Score 10 per popped pair, more for chain pops, plus a small bonus for hard drops. One play is spent when the stack overflows.

  • Left / Right — Move the falling frame.
  • Up — Rotate (swap the two frames).
  • Down — Soft drop one row.
  • X — Hard drop (locks immediately).
  • Space — Pause. Ctrl+M — Mute. Ctrl+F — Focus play.
  • A ret sitting directly on a same-color fn pops. Gravity fills the gap; chains are combos.
  • The top two rows are the danger zone. Touch the top row and the session ends.
