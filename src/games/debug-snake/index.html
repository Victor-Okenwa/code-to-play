About this game

What it is

Classic snake with a debugging twist. Steer your snake, eat red ladybugs to grow, and bank bugs in your wallet. Pink balls spawn on a timer — grab them for premium snakes. Unlock six snakes from the character shop.

Difficulty

  • Easy — Slow movement. Pink ball every 10 bugs. Speed steps up every 6 bugs.
  • Medium — Faster start. Pink ball every 8 bugs. Speed ramps every 5 bugs.
  • Hard — Fastest start. Pink ball every 6 bugs. Speed ramps every 4 bugs.

How to play

Pick a difficulty, choose a snake, then Start Debugging. One play is spent only on the final crash. Extra lives from some snakes resurrect you once without spending another play.

  • Arrow keys — Steer. You cannot reverse into yourself.
  • Space — Pause or resume.
  • Ctrl+M — Mute. Ctrl+F — Focus play.
  • Ladybugs add bugs to your wallet. Pink balls vanish after 5 seconds.

How to Play

Controls

  • Arrow Keys — Move snake
  • Left / Right — Cycle characters
  • Space — Pause/Resume
  • Ctrl+M — Toggle sound
  • Ctrl+F — Toggle focus play mode

Rules

  • Catch red ladybugs to grow and earn wallet bugs
  • Pink balls appear every N bugs (10/8/6) and expire after 5 seconds
  • Buy snakes with bugs or pink balls; Red and Robot add speed or lives
  • Hit a wall or your tail and you lose a life — final crash spends a play
